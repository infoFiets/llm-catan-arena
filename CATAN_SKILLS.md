# Catan Strategy Guide for LLM Players

## Objective
First player to reach the victory point (VP) target wins.

## Victory Point Sources
| Source | VP | How to Get |
|--------|-----|-----------|
| Settlement | 1 | Build with: wood + brick + wheat + sheep |
| City | 2 | Upgrade settlement with: 3 wheat + 2 ore |
| Longest Road | 2 | Have 5+ connected roads (and more than anyone else) |
| Largest Army | 2 | Play 3+ knight cards (and more than anyone else) |
| VP Dev Card | 1 | Keep hidden until you win |

## Building Costs
- **Road**: 1 wood + 1 brick
- **Settlement**: 1 wood + 1 brick + 1 wheat + 1 sheep
- **City**: 3 wheat + 2 ore
- **Development Card**: 1 ore + 1 wheat + 1 sheep

## Critical Strategy by Game Phase

### Early Game (0-4 VP)
- Place initial settlements on diverse numbers (6, 8, 5, 9 are best)
- Build roads toward good expansion spots
- Get access to all 5 resource types if possible

### Mid Game (4-7 VP)
- **PRIORITIZE CITIES** - They're worth 2 VP and double your resource income
- Buy development cards for knights (largest army) and hidden VP
- Secure longest road if you have wood + brick access

### End Game (7-9 VP) - CRITICAL FOR FAST WINS
When you're 1-3 VP from winning, focus ONLY on closing:

1. **If you have 3 wheat + 2 ore**: BUILD A CITY immediately
2. **If you have wood + brick + wheat + sheep**: BUILD A SETTLEMENT immediately
3. **If you're 1 road from longest road**: BUILD THAT ROAD
4. **If you have 2 knights and a knight card**: PLAY IT for largest army
5. **If you have VP dev cards and enough points to win**: You've already won!

### End Game Trading Priority
When close to winning, trade aggressively:
- 4:1 port trades are worth it to close out
- Don't hoard resources - convert them to VP

### Common End Game Mistakes to Avoid
- DON'T keep buying dev cards when you could build
- DON'T build roads that don't help you win
- DON'T end your turn if you can build something that gives VP
- DON'T forget to count your VP dev cards

## The #1 Rule
**If you can take an action that increases your VP, DO IT.**
Don't end your turn with resources that could become VP.
